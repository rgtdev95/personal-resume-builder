# Resume Builder & Tracker

A single-user local career workspace for storing a profile and detailed work history, tracking applications, visualizing hiring outcomes, and generating a tailored resume and cover letter for a job posting.

## Audience and use

The owner uses the app at a desk on a wide monitor. Prioritize scanning opportunities, reading job postings, choosing relevant experience, and accessing saved documents. Retain useful layouts on laptops and phones.

## Product constraints

- React, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, Radix/shadcn controls, Geist Variable, Lucide icons, and ECharts.
- Express and SQLite backend; existing resource endpoints and generation behavior are preserved.
- Application stages follow the backend status taxonomy; no invented data or completion claims.
- Generated document viewing and printing live at `/document.html`; this redesign covers the React workspace.
- Section switching must preserve in-progress drafts.

## Confirmed visual preference

Light and focused: warm neutral surfaces, forest-green accents, clear data tables. Confirmed by the user on 2026-10-29.
